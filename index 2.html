Who I am

I'm a cybersecurity student focused on the fundamentals that actually hold a network together: routing, protocols, hardening, and the discipline to check your own work twice.

I hold my CCNA and CompTIA Security+ certifications, and I spend most of my free time in a home lab — building networks in Packet Tracer, standing up Active Directory, and pulling apart traffic in Wireshark until I understand exactly what's happening on the wire.

Before this, I worked at UPS, where reliability wasn't optional — packages either moved correctly or they didn't. That same standard carries into how I approach security work now: attention to detail, consistency under pressure, and no shortcuts on the things that matter.

I'm drawn to the intersection of networking, automation, and security — writing Python to do in seconds what used to take an afternoon, and using that time back to actually understand the system I'm protecting.
